# Coursebuilder Materials And Compositions Inventory

Date: 2026-05-26

## Working Rule

Materials are the atomic things teachers place, cite, explain, display, annotate, or combine.

Compositions are assembled learning surfaces made from materials and other cards. They may be passive, active, simulated, playful, collaborative, assessed, or layout-driven.

Saved is not a type. Saved is a view of reusable cards.

## Taxonomy Notes

- Keep Materials noun-based and media/object-based.
- Keep Compositions noun-based and product/surface-based.
- Do not create top-level groups for verbs such as Practice, Assign, Respond, or Analyze.
- Do not create top-level groups for pedagogy such as Activity, Experience, or Resource.
- Put action into lesson templates, card configuration, prompts, and composition behavior.
- Let Atlas rely heavily on Materials, especially Source excerpt, Citation, Glossary term, Person, Place, Event, Institution, Artifact, and Case file.
- Let Canvas rely heavily on Compositions, especially Worksheet, Lab, Case study, Data investigation, Project brief, and Assessment packet.
